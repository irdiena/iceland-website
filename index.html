OUR STORY

From free time
to a sweet little passion.

IceLand started in 2026 as a small home-based idea created to fill our free time. What began as a simple activity slowly grew into a passion for making ice candy that we were proud to share.

Today, IceLand brings together nostalgic favourites, creative combinations and creamy homemade textures in one collection. Our goal is simple: make affordable treats that can turn an ordinary day into a sweeter one.

“A little treat can make a big difference in a day.”
